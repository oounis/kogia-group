CLAMP WARS is a new strategic battlefield.

This contract defines a fully playable local two-player v0.1: special selection, founding, legal movement, interception capture, forced chains, sealed-siege wins, competitive clocks, and replay-ready match data.

Build boundary Create clamp-wars from an empty project. Keep previous game repositories untouched. Do not ship fake multiplayer or fake AI.
