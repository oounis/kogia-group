# V2 generation prompts and provenance

Mode: built-in OpenAI image generation using local reference images for family/style continuity. Raster candidates were produced on chroma-magenta boards and extracted locally to 512×512 RGBA PNGs.

The Kogia logo was not used as an edit target and was not redrawn.

## Refined avatars

References: the approved six-character board and the first eight-candidate board.

Prompt contract: preserve the premium editorial Kogia family while correcting narrow silhouettes, repeated torsos, weak expressions, color collisions, and glasses/eye anatomy. Produce Salim, Jojo, Zeno, Amin, Nour, Rami, Malik, and Tala in an exact 4×2 grid on a 3:2 landscape canvas. Use broad rounded-square pygmy-sperm-whale heads, continuous head/body anatomy, cream organic lower muzzle, dark marine outline, subtle tonal depth, one personality accessory, distinct collars, and expression through brow/eye/head tilt. Background: flat `#FF00FF`; no text, logos, letters, scenery, or watermark.

The final landscape correction explicitly targeted 72–78% cell-width coverage and 82–88% cell-height coverage. The normalized outputs measure approximately 331–352 px in visible width.

## Universal reactions

References: approved reaction board and the refined V2 avatar family.

Prompt contract: exactly twelve meanings in a 4×3 board—agree, celebrate, concern, disagree, evidence-check, inspired, laugh, offer-help, question, surprised, thanks, thoughtful. Make the head and upper flippers occupy 75–82% of each cell, use large expressions and a continuous dark outline, and keep each meaning distinguishable at 32 px. Use Kogia-world semantics such as sonar, a knowledge pearl, an idea cloud, bubbles, surfacing, and offering help. One universal ocean-blue set; no product recolors.
