# Kharbga V1 prompt record

Built-in image generation was used for three boards, followed by a background-only corrective edit and local chroma extraction.

## Warrior pieces

Exactly twelve original paired Kharbga sculptures: Footguard, Sand Rider, Oracle, Citadel Guard, War Captain and Clan Elder in Sand/Night. Premium 2.5D board-game readability, stable bases, distinct head-and-shoulder silhouettes, warm limestone versus charcoal basalt, indigo and copper. Sixty percent imagined traditional Kharbga war-council craft and forty percent Kogia rounded intelligence. No copied Chess.com or Staunton shapes, European crowns, Roman armour, Egyptian pharaoh imagery, labels or logos.

## War-council profiles

Eight expressive human opponents: Amina, Massin, Zahra, Nadir, Salma, Yuba, Tarek and Lila. Friendly competitive 2.5D portrait finish, distinct ages, gazes and personalities at 48 px. Respectful original North-African/desert world rather than a claim of archaeological costume accuracy. Kogia appears through indigo/copper color, curiosity and one playful inventor—not whale costumes.

## Reactions

Eight circular reaction medallions: Respect, Brilliant Move, Clever Trap, Surprise, Thinking, Rematch, Good Game and Challenge. Human expressions and hands in a shared copper/indigo frame; no generic yellow emoji, text, weapons or copied platform reactions.

Each board was regenerated onto flat chroma magenta before being cut into 512×512 RGBA delivery files.
