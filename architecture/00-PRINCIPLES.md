# 00 — Principles

These are the rules. They exist because Kogia has already made each of these
mistakes once.

## 1. Structure is built around human domains, not products

Kogia is organised around what people do — learn, build capability, play, work —
not around the software that happens to serve them this year. A product can fail
without leaving a hole in the company.

## 2. A new idea does not create a new division

A division exists only when there is an independent business area **with users,
with value, and with a path to revenue**. Anything short of that is an experiment.

## 3. A new idea does not create a repository

The order is:

```
Idea → Research → Problem verified → Landing page → First 10 users → THEN code
```

Not: domain → logo → repository → two months of building → zero users.

**Evidence:** on 2026-09-12 fourteen repositories were deleted that contained a
README and nothing else — soldeo, serveo, relio, planeo, lumeon, cargon, caissa,
cadreo, areneo, motora, immeon, fabreon, kogia-platform, duopic. Each was a name
for a product that was never built.

## 4. Capabilities are shared, never duplicated

Design, identity, AI, data, payments and messaging belong to the Platform. A
division does not build its own. This is what makes a second product cheaper than
the first.

## 5. AI is infrastructure, not a product

Kogia does not sell "an AI". Intelligence is a capability inside every division —
helping a child learn, matching a worker to an opportunity, tuning a challenge,
finding a document. If the answer to "what does it do?" is "it's AI", it is not a
product.

## 6. Data is the long-term asset

Not the websites, not the code. What the ecosystem learns about what people need,
learn, can do and are looking for. Collected with consent, privacy and security
from the first line — retrofitting those is not possible.

## 7. Every business unit needs a path to revenue

If it has no route to money, it is an experiment. Experiments are allowed. Calling
an experiment a business unit is not.

## 8. Do not build a social network to find users

Utility brings people. Community forms around them afterwards. Never the reverse.

## 9. Never jump brand layers

`KOGIA → Kogia Group → Business → Product → Feature`. A feature does not become a
brand. See [Brand architecture](02-BRAND-ARCHITECTURE.md).

## 10. Do not let a rewrite destroy something that works

Existing repositories are inventoried, classified and mapped before anything is
moved. Live products — kogiagroup.com, kogiakids.com, edu.kogiagroup.com — are
touched only with a verified reason and a rollback.

## 11. Do not enter a market with a generic copy of what already exists

Tunisia already has restaurant POS (Kessti, Wings, Al-Makhzan) and artisan
marketplaces (ServicePro, Service.tn, Guedli). Entering those means finding the
problem they did **not** solve — or not entering.

## 12. Do not claim legal or regulated authority you do not have

For professional-office software: Kogia manages the **workflow around** the
profession — clients, files, appointments, documents, reminders, billing. It does
not replace an official register, an official instrument, or a legal signature
without qualified legal review. This distinction is not optional.

## The commercial test

> **2026 — prove that strangers will use Kogia.**
> **2027 — prove that strangers will pay Kogia.**

Until both are true, Kogia is a company being built for its founder. After both,
it is a company with users and an economy.
