# 01 — Master structure

## The layers

```
KOGIA                    the master brand — an idea, not a company
  │
KOGIA GROUP              the parent organization — owns, funds, governs
  │
DIVISIONS                Education · Skills · Play · Business · Research
  │
KOGIA PLATFORM           the shared digital foundation
  │
SHARED SERVICES          Technology · Design · Growth · Finance · Governance
```

## Kogia Group — the parent layer

Kogia Group is **not a product**. It is the organization that creates, owns and
develops businesses. It holds the capabilities that every division would otherwise
duplicate.

```
KOGIA GROUP
├── Strategy
├── Brand
├── Technology
├── Research & Innovation
├── Data & Intelligence
├── Operations
├── Finance
├── Legal & Governance
└── Growth
```

Without this layer, each product grows its own marketing, its own design, its own
infrastructure and its own AI. That is four times the cost and four different
companies wearing one name.

```
                   KOGIA GROUP
                        │
                Shared capabilities
                        │
      ┌──────────┬──────┼──────┬──────────┐
      ▼          ▼      ▼      ▼          ▼
 Technology     AI     Data  Design     Growth
      │          │      │      │          │
      └──────────┴──────┼──────┴──────────┘
                        ▼
                All Kogia businesses
```

## The five divisions

| Division | Question it answers | Mandate |
|---|---|---|
| **Education** | What do I know? | Develop human knowledge |
| **Skills** | What can I do? | Turn human capability into opportunity |
| **Play** | How do I explore and challenge myself? | Challenge, create and connect through play |
| **Business** | How does real work actually run? | Modernize how real businesses work |
| **Research** | What is actually worth building? | Observe the world before building for it |

Education, Skills, Play and Business are **business domains** — they can hold
products and earn revenue. Research is a **capability** of the Group: it does not
sell, it decides what deserves to exist.

## Kogia Harmony — why the divisions are not separate companies

A person moves through the ecosystem. That movement is what makes "Harmony" a
structure rather than a slogan.

```
                         HUMAN
                           │
                           ▼
                         LEARN ──────────── Kogia Education
                           │
                           ▼
                        DEVELOP ─────────── Kogia Skills
                           │
                           ▼
                         APPLY ──────────── Opportunity
                           │
                           ▼
                          WORK ──────────── Kogia Business
                           │
                           ▼
                          GROW
                           │
                           ▼
                          PLAY ──────────── Kogia Play
                           │
                           ▼
                    CREATE / THINK
                           │
                           └──────► back to LEARN
```

Education does not live alone. Skills does not live alone. Play does not live
alone. Each is a phase of the same human development, and the Platform is what
carries a person between them without starting over.

## Why Play, not Games

"Games" locks the division into video games. "Play" allows digital games,
educational games, board games, puzzles, interactive experiences, competition and
family entertainment.

`Kogia Games` may still exist as a **commercial brand** for a specific product
line. At the level of architecture, the domain is Play.

## Why Business was added

Education, Skills and Play build long-term human value but earn slowly. Business
sells to people who already run a business, already feel the cost of the problem,
and already pay for solutions. It is the division with the shortest distance to
revenue — see [10 — Business opportunities](10-BUSINESS-OPPORTUNITIES.md).
