# 02 — Brand architecture

## The layers, and the rule

```
KOGIA              master brand
  ↓
Kogia Group        corporate identity
  ↓
Business brands    Kogia Education · Kogia Skills · Kogia Play · Kogia Business
  ↓
Product brands     Kogia Kids · Coreon EDU · EduPlus Connect · …
  ↓
Features           never brands
```

**You never skip a layer, and a feature never becomes a brand.**

This single rule is what prevents the state Kogia was in before 2026-09-12:
fourteen brand names, each with a repository and a description, none with a product.

## The naming test

Before anything gets a name, answer one question:

| Is it… | Then it is | Gets a name? |
|---|---|---|
| The company? | Kogia Group | Already named |
| A business area? | `Kogia X` | Yes — but only under [Principle 2](00-PRINCIPLES.md) |
| A product? | Independent name, or endorsed by Kogia | Yes, once it has users |
| A feature? | Part of a product | **No** |

If you cannot say which row it is in, it is a feature.

## Endorsement

Two valid forms:

- **Kogia-named** — `Kogia Kids`, `Kogia Skills`. Use when the product's value comes
  from being part of the ecosystem.
- **Independent, endorsed** — `Coreon EDU — by KogiaGroup`. Use when the product must
  stand alone commercially, sell into a market that does not know Kogia, or carry
  its own reputation.

Both are correct. What is not correct is inventing a third brand family.

A "planned" business brand has a place in the structure and **no domain, no logo
and no repository** until a product inside it is real.

## The visual system

The visual identity is **Kogia Harmony**, defined in
[`brand/KOGIA_HARMONY.md`](../brand/KOGIA_HARMONY.md). It is the single design
language for the whole ecosystem. Read it before touching any colour, logo or font.

Division-level visual differentiation is expressed **within** Harmony — never by
inventing a separate design language.
