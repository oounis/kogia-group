# 05 — Lifecycle

How an idea becomes a product, and how it dies. This is the process that replaces
"I had an idea, so I made a repository."

## The pipeline

```
IDEA
  ▼
KOGIA RESEARCH ──────── problem not real ────────► IDEA BANK
  ▼ problem verified
LANDING PAGE ────────── nobody signs up ─────────► KILL
  ▼ interest shown
KOGIA LABS (MVP)
  ▼
FIRST 10 USERS ──────── nobody uses it ──────────► KILL
  ▼ people use it
FIRST PAYING CUSTOMER ─ nobody pays ─────────────► KILL or keep free
  ▼ people pay
PRODUCT
  ▼
growth, retention, unit economics
  ▼
BUSINESS UNIT  (only if it is an independent business area)
```

**Code is written at the Labs stage — not before.**

## The gates

| Gate | Question | Fail = |
|---|---|---|
| Research | Does this problem exist for real people? | Idea Bank |
| Landing | Will anyone give an email for it? | Kill |
| MVP | Will 10 people use it? | Kill |
| Revenue | Will one person pay? | Kill or stay free |
| Product | Do they come back? | Fix or kill |
| Division | Independent area, users, value, revenue? | Stays a product |

**Killing is a success.** The cost of a dead experiment is a landing page. The cost
of a dead product is two months and a repository nobody dares delete.

## What each stage may have

| Stage | Name | Domain | Logo | Repository |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Idea | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Research | working title | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Landing page | working title | subdomain of kogiagroup.com | ❌ | in the group repo |
| Labs / MVP | working title | subdomain | ❌ | **one** repo, private |
| Product | real name | own domain if needed | yes | its own repo |
| Business unit | brand | yes | yes | a family of repos |

**A repository is created at the Labs stage, not before.** This single line is the
rule that would have prevented fourteen empty repositories.
