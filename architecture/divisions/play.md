# Kogia Play

> **Challenge, create and connect through play.**

"Play" rather than "Games" on purpose. Games would lock the division into video
games for twenty years. Play holds digital games, educational games, board games,
puzzles, interactive experiences, competition and family entertainment.

`Kogia Games` may exist later as a commercial brand for one product line. The
architectural domain stays Play.

## Structure

```
KOGIA PLAY
├── Games
├── Interactive Experiences
├── Learning Through Play
├── Challenges
├── Competition
├── Creativity
└── Social Play
```

## Why Play earns its place

Play is not decoration. It is where three things happen that no other division does
as well:

1. **Retention** — people return to play without being asked.
2. **Assessment without a test** — a child's reasoning shows in how they play.
3. **Reach** — a good game travels further than a good worksheet.

## Connection to the rest of Kogia

```
Kogia Education ──► Learning Through Play ──► Kogia Play
                                                   │
Kogia Skills ◄──── Challenges reveal ability ◄─────┘
```

Intelligence tunes difficulty: *"Your reasoning challenges should become slightly
harder."* That is Play doing assessment quietly.

## State today — deliberately empty

Kharbga and ClampWars were both retired on 2026-09-12. That was correct: both were
built before anyone asked for them, and neither reached users.

**Play holds no product and will not until Education and Skills have paying
customers.** The division exists in the structure so that when a game does earn its
place, it has a home — and does not become a new company.

## The rule for this division

Anything proposed for Play must answer: **who asked for this?**

If the answer is "it would be fun to build", it goes to the
[Idea Bank](../09-IDEA-BANK.md), not into code.
