# Kogia Education

> **Develop human knowledge.**

Not "software for schools". The full cycle of learning — whatever form it takes.

## Structure

```
KOGIA EDUCATION
├── Early Learning          before school; play-led
├── School Education        nursery → grade 6 and beyond
├── Learning Platforms      the software people learn on
├── Educational Operations  how a school actually runs
├── Content                 worksheets, lessons, curricula
├── Assessment              measuring what was learned
├── Teacher Enablement      tools for the person teaching
└── Lifelong Learning       adults, re-skilling, curiosity
```

Any future educational product finds its place here — software, platform, content,
service, AI tutor, or a teaching methodology. **No new division is needed.**

## Products today

| Product | Sits in | State |
|---|---|---|
| **Kogia Kids** | Early Learning + Content | Live — kogiakids.com, 96 printable sheets |
| **Coreon EDU** | Educational Operations + Assessment | Live, public repo |
| **EduPlus Connect** | Educational Operations | Live — in real school use |

## What is missing, and matters

- **Learning Passport** — a child's learning record across years, not one term.
  Feeds directly into [Kogia Skills](skills.md). ⭐ high value
- **Teacher Toolkit** — generate worksheets, lessons and activities. The natural
  commercial extension of Kogia Kids.
- **Parent Companion** — help a parent follow what their child is doing.
- **Micro Learning** — 5–10 minute lessons tied to one specific skill. This is the
  bridge into Skills.

All four are parked in the [Idea Bank](../09-IDEA-BANK.md) until a real user asks.

## Revenue

| Model | Applies to |
|---|---|
| B2B SaaS | Schools — Coreon EDU, EduPlus Connect |
| Subscription | Parents and teachers — Kids premium, Teacher Toolkit |
| Free content → funnel | Kogia Kids printables bring the audience |
| Services | Implementation and training for school customers |

**Kogia Kids is the top of the funnel for the whole division.** Free printables
attract parents and teachers; the paid products sit one step behind them.
