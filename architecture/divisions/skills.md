# Kogia Skills

> **Turn human capability into opportunity.**

This is deliberately much larger than "jobs". A job board is a listing. Kogia
Skills is the record of what a person can do, proven, and what that opens for them.

## Structure

```
KOGIA SKILLS
├── Identity          Who can you become?
├── Skills            What can you do?
├── Development       What can you learn?
├── Certification     What can you prove?
├── Opportunities     Where can your skills take you?
├── Work              Employment · projects · contracts
├── Mobility          Local and international
└── Organizations     Finding and developing talent
```

## The human journey

```
LEARN → BUILD A SKILL → PROVE IT → PROFESSIONAL IDENTITY
   → DISCOVER OPPORTUNITIES → WORK → REPUTATION → GROW
        │                                          │
        └──────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Each step is a product surface. None of them is a job board.

## The three that matter most

### 1. Skill Passport ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

A verified professional identity: skills, experience, and actual work. Part of
[Kogia Passport](../platform/kogia-passport.md).

The problem it solves: a CV says *"I am excellent at electrical work."* Nobody can
check that. A Skill Passport carries evidence.

### 2. Opportunity Radar ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Instead of the user searching, the user **describes themselves once**:

```
Tunisian · Electrician · 8 years · French + Arabic
Interested in: France / Italy / Gulf
Minimum salary: X · Relocation: yes
```

Intelligence works in the background — gathering opportunities, reading
requirements, comparing them to the profile, scoring the match.

```
Your Radar
  94%  Industrial Electrician — Italy
  89%  Electrical Technician — Saudi Arabia
  83%  Maintenance Technician — France
```

**This is the answer to "why would anyone come back to the site?"** The opportunities
change every day. The user does not have to.

### 3. Kogia Proof ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Evidence that a skill is real, from several independent sources:

```
Assessment · Certificates · Employer verification
Portfolio · Completed jobs · Customer ratings
        │
        ▼
   Kogia Skill Score
```

⚠️ The score must be **decomposable and disputable**. A number a person cannot see
inside, cannot challenge, and that comes from a model alone, is not proof — it is
a liability. Every component must be visible and appealable.

**Where the money is:** a company needing 20 HVAC technicians does not want 3,000
CVs. It wants *"142 verified candidates match your requirements."* Companies pay
for that.

## The loop that makes Harmony real

Opportunity Radar finds a 72% match. Why not 90%?

```
❌ Missing: HVAC Level II certification
❌ English requirement
✓ Experience   ✓ Location   ✓ Availability
```

> *You are two skills away from qualifying.*

→ Learn in [Kogia Education](education.md) → prove it with Kogia Proof →
**match becomes 91%.**

This is the single strongest long-term idea in the ecosystem: Education and Skills
each make the other more valuable.

## Also in scope

- **Trade Network** — electricians, mechanics, HVAC, plumbers. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ — connects
  directly to [Kogia Business](business.md): Skills finds the professional,
  Business runs their business.
- **Kogia Missions** — short, completable units instead of long courses. Crosses
  Education, Skills and Play.
