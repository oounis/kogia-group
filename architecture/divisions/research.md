# Kogia Research

> **Observe the world before building for it.**

Research is a **capability of the Group**, not a commercial division. It does not
sell. It decides what deserves to exist — and, just as importantly, what does not.

```
KOGIA RESEARCH

```

## Its two jobs

### 1. Stop Kogia building the wrong thing

Every product proposal passes through Research first:

- Does this problem exist for real people?
- Who already solves it, and how well?
- Is there a gap, or would we be shipping a generic copy?
- Would anyone pay?

**This has already earned its place.** Research into Tunisia found that restaurants
are well served (Kessti, Wings, Al-Makhzan) and artisan marketplaces are crowded
(ServicePro, Service.tn, Guedli) — while professional offices still run on paper.
That single finding redirected the whole Business division.

### 2. Bring people to Kogia

Research output is public, useful, and free. It is the top of the funnel:

```
Research → Articles → Google / Reddit / Social → Kogia → Products
```

Kogia becomes a company that produces **knowledge**, not only software. Knowledge
is cited, shared and linked. Software is not.

## Kogia Index — the sharpest instrument

Free, recurring, public indicators built from public information:

```
Tunisian Skills Opportunity Index
  Saudi Arabia ↑   HVAC demand: high
  Italy        ↑   Construction demand: growing
  France       →   Industrial maintenance: stable
```

Others: *Skills Most Requested in GCC*, *Education Technology Index*.

Why this works: it is findable on Google, discussable on Reddit, quotable by a
journalist, readable by companies, shareable by workers. And it ends with one line —

> *Want opportunities matched to your profile? Join Kogia Skills.*

⚠️ Every index must state its sources and method. An index nobody can verify is
marketing, and it will be treated as such.

## Kogia Labs — where verified problems become experiments

```
IDEA → RESEARCH → problem verified? ──no──► Idea Bank
                        │yes
                        ▼
                   KOGIA LABS
                        ▼
                       MVP
                        ▼
                  FIRST 10 USERS ──none──► kill
                        ▼
                  FIRST CUSTOMER ──none──► kill
                        ▼
                    PRODUCT
```

Labs is an incubator, not a division. Its products carry no brand, no domain and no
public identity until they leave it.

**Labs exists because of a specific failure pattern:** an idea became a domain, a
logo, a repository and two months of building, and reached zero users — fourteen
times over. Labs inverts the order.

## Revenue

Research does not sell directly. It earns through:

- **Leads** into Skills, Business and Education
- **B2B reports** for companies that need the data
- **Authority** — the reason a customer trusts Kogia at all
