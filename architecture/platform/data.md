# Kogia Data

> The long-term asset is not the websites and not the code. It is what the ecosystem
> learns.

```
KOGIA DATA
├── Learning Intelligence      what people learn, and where they struggle
├── Skills Intelligence        what people can do
├── Opportunity Intelligence   what the market asks for
├── Engagement Intelligence    what people actually use
├── Market Intelligence        where the gaps are
└── Product Intelligence       what works and what fails
```

## Why it compounds

The questions Kogia can answer get better as it grows:

- What do people want?
- What are they learning?
- Which skills are in demand?
- Where are the opportunities?
- What works, and what fails?

A company that can answer those builds better products than one that guesses. This
is also what makes [Kogia Research](../divisions/research.md) credible — it reports
from data rather than opinion.

## Non-negotiable, from the first line

**Privacy, consent and security are designed in at the start.** They cannot be
retrofitted — not technically, and not legally.

1. **Consent is per purpose.** Using Kogia Kids is not consent to be profiled for
   employers.
2. **Children's data is a separate class**, with stricter rules and a shorter
   retention period.
3. **Aggregate by default.** Research and indices publish patterns, never
   individuals.
4. **Deletion is real.** If a person deletes their account, the data goes —
   including from derived datasets.
5. **Retention is stated, per dataset.** "Forever" is not a retention policy.

## State

**Not built.** Today each product holds its own data. That is acceptable now, and it
is the thing to get right before the second product in a division ships.
