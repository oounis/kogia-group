# 10 — Business opportunities

Where Kogia Business should look first. Tunisia is the starting market because it is
the one that can be reached directly, without a sales team.

## The method

> Find industries that still run on **paper, phone, WhatsApp and Excel** — then check
> whether someone has already solved it well.

The second half is what stops Kogia shipping a generic copy.

## What the research already found

### ✅ Legal and professional offices — the strongest opening

- Tunisia's Ministry of Justice is itself moving toward digital justice, piloting
  electronic exchange of judicial documents and remote civil hearings, and asking
  lawyers to create electronic accounts.
- Meanwhile **عدول الإشهاد** (notaries public) still carry heavy paper procedure:
  official instruments, dedicated registers, copies, registration, dates — all
  legally structured.
- Their own national body lists digitisation among its stated aims.

**The gap:** the state is digitising the *court*. Nobody is digitising the *office* —
clients, files, appointments, documents, reminders, search, archiving, billing.

⚠️ **The boundary:** Kogia builds the workflow around the profession. It does not
replace an official register, an official instrument, or a legal signature without
qualified legal review. See [Kogia Business](divisions/business.md).

### ❌ Restaurants — already served

Kessti (POS, kitchen, orders, QR, reservations, loyalty), Wings (restaurant ERP),
Al-Makhzan (POS, kitchen, stock, food cost, QR, supplier invoices), Menu-QR, Scanny.

Entering with another POS would be a generic copy. **The only way in is a problem
they did not solve** — workforce, supplier marketplace, equipment maintenance, food
waste, temporary staff, compliance.

### ❌ Generic artisan marketplace — already served

ServicePro, Service.tn, Guedli already connect plumbers, electricians and artisans
to customers.

**But:** they connect. They do not *run the business*. Kogia Skills finds the
professional; Kogia Business runs the professional's business. That is a different
product, and no one holds both ends.

## The automotive example — why it is attractive

A garage and a mechanic sit on both sides of Kogia:

```
Mechanic  → Kogia Skill Profile   (Skills)
Garage    → Kogia Business Workspace  (Business)

Vehicle: Toyota · 2018 · 120,000 km
History: oil change · brake pads · battery · AC · tires · next service
```

The garage gets an operating system, the mechanic gets a verifiable record, the
customer gets history that follows the car. Three parties, one ecosystem.

## ⚠️ The next step is interviews, not code

Before a single line of a legal-office SaaS:

**Talk to 10–20 real professionals.** See the actual workflow — what takes the most
time, what they already pay for, what they would refuse to change, and where the law
constrains the process.

The scores above are hypotheses. Interviews turn them into facts, or kill them
cheaply. That is the [lifecycle](05-LIFECYCLE.md) working as intended.
