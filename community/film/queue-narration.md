## Notes for whoever records it

- One calm voice, no music bed louder than the voice.
- The film says on its last frame that it illustrates a platform in development. Do not add claims of clients or results.
- Services shown are activity licences, building permits and attestations, which are municipal services in Libya. Civil status documents are issued by the Civil Registry Authority there.
