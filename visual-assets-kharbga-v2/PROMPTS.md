Eight contemporary, cross-generational custodians: Board Builder, Counter, Market Challenger, Pattern Teacher, Storykeeper, Young Learner, Café Champion and Quiet Observer. Painterly 2.5D portraits grounded in a living Tunisian/North-African community rather than a fictional ancient court. No weapons, armour, fantasy or costume caricature.

Eight medallions based on actual game meaning: Respect, Trap, Captured, Chain Capture, Safe Haff, Exchange, Blocked and Rematch. Tactile sand, pebbles, date pits and hands replace generic emojis.
